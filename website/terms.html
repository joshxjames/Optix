4. The service

4.1 The desktop app

The Optix desktop app is a floating widget that runs on your machine and assists you with three modes:

  • Ask — single-shot Q&A over a screenshot of your active window. No actions on your behalf, no file access.
  • Access — agent loop. The app can click, type, scroll, run shell commands, and read or write files within a workspace folder you choose. Subject to the approval mode you set.
  • Automate — Access plus recording. A task you run once becomes a routine you can replay.

The desktop app supports two paths: BYO key (you supply an Anthropic, OpenAI, Kimi, or Gemini key — usage is billed by that provider, not by us), and Optix Cloud (you subscribe to us; we forward your requests to Anthropic on our admin key).

4.2 Optix Cloud

Optix Cloud is currently offered in two tiers:

  • Starter — USD $49 / month, 5,000,000 tokens / month, all three modes, web search included.
  • Pro — USD $99 / month, 15,000,000 tokens / month, everything in Starter, priority support.

Optix Cloud uses Anthropic's Claude models (Opus, Sonnet, Haiku). Switching providers (OpenAI / Kimi / Gemini) is only available on the BYO-key path; Optix Cloud doesn't relay those.

6. Subscriptions, billing, and cancellation

6.1 How billing works

Optix Cloud subscriptions are processed by Stripe. When you subscribe, Stripe collects your payment method directly on its hosted Checkout page; we never see your card number. Pricing is in USD. The billing cycle is monthly, anchored to your signup date (not the calendar month).

6.2 Token allowance

Your monthly token allowance resets on the first day of each calendar month (UTC), independent of your billing cycle. Reaching the allowance triggers a monthly_allowance_exceeded error from the relay; your subscription stays active and the next reset restores your access. We pre-reserve a small buffer (50,000 tokens) at the start of each request to prevent concurrent requests from racing past the cap.

6.3 Plan changes (upgrade / downgrade)

You can change tier at any time via the in-app billing portal or by contacting us. Stripe prorates the fee — you pay the difference for the rest of the current period or get a credit for unused time.

6.4 Cancellation

You can cancel from the Stripe Customer Portal (Settings → Manage Billing in the desktop app) or by emailing admin@covetable.com.au. Cancellations take effect at the end of the current paid period — you keep access until then, and we don't issue partial refunds for unused days. You can reactivate before the period ends to keep your subscription rolling.

6.5 Failed payments

If your card fails, your subscription enters past_due. Stripe automatically retries on its standard schedule. While past-due, your relay access is suspended (you can still use BYO key). If payment isn't recovered within Stripe's retry window (typically up to 30 days), the subscription is cancelled and your access ends.

6.6 Refunds

We don't issue refunds for partial months or unused tokens. If you believe a charge was made in error, email us — we review case-by-case.
